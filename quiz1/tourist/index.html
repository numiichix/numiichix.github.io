Tourist places in Bandung

Gedung Sate

A landmark built in 1920 and now the office of the West Java Governor. Its roof ornament looks like a skewer of satay, which gave the building its name. Part of it is open as a museum.

Jl. Diponegoro No. 22, Citarum, Bandung Wetan, Kota Bandung

Trans Studio Bandung

One of the largest indoor theme parks in Indonesia, with rides, shows, and themed zones. Good for a rainy day or a family trip.

Jl. Gatot Subroto No. 289, Cibangkong, Batununggal, Kota Bandung

Gunung Tangkuban Perahu

An active volcano north of Bandung with a wide crater you can walk around. Its name means "upturned boat", from the Sangkuriang legend. Expect cool air and a light sulfur smell.

Cikole, Lembang, Kabupaten Bandung Barat
